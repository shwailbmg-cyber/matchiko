# Similar businesses worldwide: what they offer

Research for Matchiko (October 2026, updated November 2026). The main
competition is still WhatsApp groups, phone calls and Facebook pages, but
Algerian apps now exist: **Matchna** (book a pitch, pay at the venue or by
card, QR check-in; cancellation rules left to each venue; no user reviews
found) and **FieldZ** ("coming soon"). Morocco and Tunisia have KoraKick,
FootConnect, MatchUp and MyMatch.tn (marketing pages only). See the
November section at the end.

## Overview

| Business (market) | Type | What they offer | How they earn | What we take |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Playtomic** (padel/tennis, Europe + MENA, ~1M monthly users) | Booking + open matches + club software | Book courts, open matches, levels, payments split per player, academy lessons, leagues/tournaments, club manager software. **Premium players**: no booking fees, advanced stats, alerts for last-minute spots and free courts | Commission on every booking + software fee for clubs + player Premium subscription. €29M net revenue (company figure) | Commission + club tools + Premium player, exactly like our M2–M5. Last-minute spot alerts (our B1 push) |
| **Malaeb** (football, Bahrain/KSA/Kuwait/UAE/Oman, 150k players, 400+ venues) | Football booking + community | Find and book pitches, create teams, challenge other teams, join games (14 players), profiles with stats and reviews | Not public (booking volume ~$1M in its first 15 months) | Closest model to ours in the Arab world. Team challenges (J5), profiles with stats |
| **Hudle** (multi-sport, India) | Marketplace + venue software (SaaS) | Players book venues, join pick-up games and leagues, learn a sport ("Hudle Play" sessions). Venues get booking, invoicing, payments and memberships software, plus on-site facility management | Venue software subscriptions + bookings | Venue software as a product on its own (calendar, walk-ins, memberships). | **KheloMore** (multi-sport, India) | Booking marketplace | "Guaranteed booking", **split payment**, refer & earn, deals and discounts, filters by time/amenities/reviews, coaching programmes | Bookings / deals | Split payment = our L2 share per player. Refer & earn = our I7. Coaching = Premium perk |
| **Playo** (multi-sport, India, 3M users) | Players + venues | Find partners by time and skill, host games, book venues, karma points redeemable on bookings | Commissions + subscriptions | Coins redeemable on pitch discounts (our store) |

| **Footy Addicts** (football, UK) | Pay-to-play pickup | Organisers host recurring games, pay online to secure a spot, credit refunds with 24 h notice, reliability score, no-show marking | Per-player game fees | Reliability % (F1), hosts for recurring games (C10) |
| **Fubles** (football, Italy) | Pickup community | One-click join, rate teammates and opponents after each game, stats profile. Free for players | Partnerships with sports centres | Post-game ratings (H3), free for players (M1) |
| **Plei** (pickup soccer, USA/LatAm, 200k users, 180 facilities) | Pickup platform | Find and join pickup games, reserve spots, works with facilities | Spot fees + facilities | Facility partnerships in several cities |
| **OpenSports** (organisers, North America) | Tools for organisers | Pickup games, leagues, tournaments, classes, memberships/passes, waitlists, waivers, chat, recurring events | Software for organisers/facilities | Waitlists (C6), recurring events (C10), memberships (E6 "abonnement") |
| **Spond** (team management, Europe) | Team admin | Season events, one-tap RSVP, automatic reminders, payments per fixture, line-ups, goals, player of the match | Payment fees | RSVP + reminders (C7), who-paid list (L3), MVP vote (H2) |
| **Anybuddy** (France, backed by Decathlon) | Court booking | Geolocated free courts across many clubs, booking; Decathlon adds gear and partner-finding | Bookings | A sports retailer as a sponsor/partner (M4): gear in the store, sponsored tournaments |
| **Decathlon Play** (Brazil) | Pickup organiser | Create events, find nearby players by distance, skill level, days and times, in-app chat | Free (brand marketing) | Filters by level/day/time (D2) |
| **Stadion 24/7** (Uzbekistan) | Pitch booking | Find and book football and mini-football pitches, **online or cash payment** | Bookings | Proof that cash-first booking apps work in similar markets |

## What nobody does well (our edge)

1. **Cash-market trust.** Others rely on card prepayment to stop no-shows.
   We use in-person ID checks, reliability %, the 10-hour confirmation and
   QR cash check-in. That fits Algeria, where most people pay cash.
2. **Smart matching for football groups.** Solo / friends / whole team, with
   positions ranked by preference. Most apps only list open games.
3. **Manager tools that replace the notebook** (walk-ins, weekly
   abonnements, Ramadan hours), free on the commission plan.
4. **Local fit:** 69 wilayas and small towns, Friday–Saturday weekend,
   Ramadan mode, Arabic/French/English.

## Ideas from this research (all accepted ✅, now E7, E16, E18, E19 in FEATURES.md)

- **Last-minute deals** (Playtomic/KheloMore): an empty slot within 3 hours
  gets a discount pushed to nearby players. Fills empty slots for the venue.
- **Training sessions / beginners' sessions** (Hudle Play, KheloMore
  coaching): venues or coaches list paid sessions. Also a Premium perk.
- **Student / off-peak prices** (UrbanSoccer): a price type in E7.
- **Equipment deposit** (UrbanSoccer): bibs and ball against a deposit (E16).

## November 2026: game organising, user feedback, sharing

Second research pass, used for `docs/GAMES.md`. Most pages could not be
opened directly from the research session, so this comes from search
results of help centres, store listings, review sites and press; treat
figures as reported.

| App (market) | Mechanic worth knowing |
|---|---|
| **Playtomic** | Open match with a level band; players outside it "request a place". Friendly vs competitive locked at booking; only competitive moves the level. Reliability figure on levels. "10-Match Challenge" to re-check a level |
| **CeleBreak** (Spain, Germany, Dubai) | Paid host brings balls and bibs and rebalances teams; friends who join together are **not** guaranteed the same side. Leagues accept individuals and assign them a team |
| **FiveKick** (Belgium) | Teams post when they are free; others challenge. One side enters the score, the other confirms, Elo updates |
| **CricHeroes** (India), **OLE** (UAE) | One "Looking for" board: opponent, player, team, ground, umpire. OLE: invite a referee in the app |
| **BenchApp Finder** | Captain posts a missing spot with position, level and money terms; nearby free agents are notified |
| **Powerleague** (UK) | "Ringerz" stand-in pool for same-day dropouts; says 84 % of 5-a-side teams suffer them. "Transfer Market" for players without a team |
| **Volo, Urban Rec, Leisure Leagues** | Solo players ("free agents") merged into teams at league registration |
| **Goleiro de Aluguel** (Brazil) | Goalkeeper for hire, rated on punctuality, behaviour, technique |
| **Atmos Football**, **Poteau** (France) | Balanced teams with win probability; level from a situational questionnaire; each game shows its expected level |
| **Appito / Chega+** (Brazil), **Tonsser** | Peer ratings and awards after the game (best player, scorer, defender); attendance and waiting lists |
| **Spond** | Attendance cap, waiting list that promotes itself, automatic reminders to non-responders |
| **Strava** | "Local Legend" (most efforts in 90 days), private group challenges, stat stickers for Stories |
| **Yalla Hagz** (Egypt) | Small online deposit, rest at the pitch. Main reported failure: venue calendar out of sync with the app |
| **Malaeb** (Gulf) | Unpaid (cash) bookings cancel free, prepaid do not, so users learn not to prepay |

Not found anywhere: an app that builds a team of solo players on demand
to face an existing team for a single match (Matchiko Select, GAMES § 3.1).

Top user complaints across these apps: unfair cancellation/refund rules;
no-shows and short-handed games; skill mismatch and unfair levels;
unreliable hosts and venues; payment bugs; no human support; toxic play.
Top wishes: player ratings, games by level, a free pass for a late
cancel, rewards, calendar sync, instant spot alerts, friends features.
